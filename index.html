About Allie

Writer. Reviewer. Podcaster. Permanently geeking out about fictional worlds — currently deep in writing an epic romantasy-dystopian series of my own, and podcasting weekly about everything I'm reading, watching, and playing.

I started My Fictional Adventure because I could never talk about books, shows, manga, and games in just one place — my brain doesn't separate them, so why should the shelf? This room is that: one cozy spot for all of it, organized the way I actually think about it.

No last names, no personal details here — just Allie, and everything I'm currently obsessed with. Come sit by the lamp for a while.
